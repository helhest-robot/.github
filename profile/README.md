# Helhest Tactical Ground Robot 🤖

**Helhest** is a Czech company developing cutting-edge **tactical unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs)** designed for military, security, and rescue operations.

---

## 🌐 About Us

Helhest builds highly capable tactical ground robots engineered for real-world operational environments. Our platforms combine robust hardware design with advanced autonomy and remote-control capabilities, enabling forces to perform dangerous missions without putting personnel at risk.

Named after the mythological *helhest* — a three-legged horse from Scandinavian folklore — our robots are built to go where humans cannot.

---

## 🤖 Our Platform

Helhest tactical ground robots are designed to handle a wide range of missions, including:

- **Reconnaissance & surveillance** — extended-range ISR in complex terrain
- **Force protection** — perimeter security and threat detection
- **CBRN operations** — operations in chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear environments
- **Logistics & resupply** — autonomous cargo transport in contested areas
- **Explosive ordnance disposal (EOD)** — safe standoff distance during EOD tasks
- **Search & rescue** — support in disaster or combat search and rescue scenarios

---

## 🏭 Engineering & Design

Our robots are built with a focus on:

- **Rugged, all-terrain mobility** for demanding operational environments
- **Modular payload architecture** allowing rapid mission reconfiguration
- **Low acoustic and thermal signature** for covert operations
- **Remote and autonomous operation** with intuitive operator interfaces
- **NATO STANAG compatibility** for interoperability with allied forces
